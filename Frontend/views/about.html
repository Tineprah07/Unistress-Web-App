What is UniStress?

UniStress is a comprehensive wellbeing management platform designed specifically for university students. University life brings a unique set of challenges, from exam pressure and assignment deadlines to managing sleep, staying active, and maintaining mental health. UniStress brings all of these together in one place, giving students a simple, intuitive way to track, understand, and improve their daily wellbeing.

Rather than relying on scattered apps for different aspects of health, UniStress provides a unified dashboard where students can monitor their stress levels, log exercise and sleep, track water intake, use focus timers for productive study sessions, practise guided breathing exercises, journal their thoughts, and set reminders - all from a single, beautifully designed interface.

Why Was It Built?

Research consistently shows that university students experience higher levels of stress, anxiety, and burnout compared to the general population. Many students struggle silently, unsure of how to manage their wellbeing or unaware of how their daily habits impact their mental and physical health.

UniStress was built to bridge that gap - to provide students with an accessible, non-judgmental tool that encourages self-awareness and healthier habits. By making wellbeing tracking effortless and visual, the app empowers students to take proactive control of their health during one of the most demanding periods of their lives.

UniStress was developed by Augustine Gyamprah as a Final Year Project for the completion of a degree at the University of Portsmouth. This project represents the culmination of years of learning in software development, user experience design, and web technologies.

The idea for UniStress was born out of first-hand experience with the pressures of university life and a genuine desire to create something meaningful - a tool that could make a real difference in the lives of fellow students. Every feature, from the stress tracker to the breathing exercises, was carefully researched and designed with the student experience at its core.

Final Year Project

This application was developed as part of the Final Year Project module, which is a core requirement for undergraduate students completing their degree programme. The project involved the full software development lifecycle - from initial research and requirements gathering, through design and prototyping, to implementation, testing, and deployment.

The goal of the project was to investigate the challenges university students face in managing their wellbeing and to develop a practical, evidence-informed web application that addresses those challenges. UniStress demonstrates proficiency in full-stack web development, responsive UI/UX design, RESTful API architecture, third-party API integration, user authentication, and data visualisation.
